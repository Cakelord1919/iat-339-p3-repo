02 / BIO

Designing the space
between image and play.

I am Eric Gabriel Cheng Li, a game designer, graphic designer, and 3D artist. I build environments, images, and interactions as connected parts of an experience. My process moves between spatial planning, visual systems, modelling, and playtesting, using each discipline to make the next decision clearer. I am most interested in projects where atmosphere must also communicate: a space should establish a feeling, guide attention, and give the player a reason to act.
